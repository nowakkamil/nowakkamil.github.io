Privacy notice

Last updated: 11 August 2026

This notice explains how I handle personal data when you contact me through this website. The data controller is Kamil Nowak. You can contact me at contact@nowakkamil.com.

Data I collect and why

The contact form collects your name, email address, and message so that I can review and respond to your enquiry. I process this data to take steps at your request before entering into a contract where relevant (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), and otherwise on the basis of my legitimate interest in responding to professional enquiries (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Providing this information is voluntary, but I cannot respond through the form without it. I do not use contact-form data for marketing or automated decision-making.

Service providers and transfers

Cloudflare provides hosting and abuse protection for the form, including Turnstile, and Resend processes the email delivery. These providers process data on my behalf. Where data is processed outside the European Economic Area, I rely on a lawful transfer mechanism and appropriate safeguards as required by the GDPR.

How long I keep it

I keep enquiries only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond and manage any resulting professional relationship, normally no longer than 12 months after our last correspondence. I may retain limited information for longer where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask me to:

  • give you access to your personal data;
  • correct or delete it;
  • restrict or object to its processing; or
  • provide data you supplied in a portable format.

You may object at any time to processing based on legitimate interests. You can also lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO) in Poland or with the supervisory authority where you live or work.

Cookieless analytics

This site may use cookieless, aggregate analytics to understand traffic and site performance. It does not use analytics cookies. Analytics are separate from the contact form and are not used to build advertising profiles from the details you submit.
